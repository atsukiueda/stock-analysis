## 1. Purpose and scope

This Procedure defines the controlled lifecycle for project-level documentation within Documentation Governance scope: intake, draft, independent review, revision, approval, release, and maintenance. Existing `KnowledgeBase/00_Project` authorities remain outside this Procedure unless an approved migration explicitly changes that relationship.

## 2. Principles

Draft material is noncanonical. Review exists to find defects. An author cannot independently approve their own work. Approval precedes release. One governed subject has one effective canonical publication.

## 3. Procedure

1. Identify the proposed document’s class, owner, scope, dependencies, and governing authority.
2. Create a controlled Draft that states its status and does not claim effective authority.
3. Route the Draft to an independent reviewer with appropriate scope knowledge.
4. Preserve findings and revise the Draft or record a reasoned disposition.
5. Route the revised artifact to the authority identified by the Project Constitution for approval or rejection.
6. Upon approval, create the effective canonical publication and preserve the relationship to its approved source and applicable approval and release evidence.
7. Maintain the publication by routing material changes through this workflow; preserve history and do not silently replace authority.

## 4. Change, migration, and retirement

Any move, rename, consolidation, supersession, or retirement affecting an existing Knowledge Base authority requires an approved migration before implementation. This Procedure does not define migration methods, record schemas, or retention periods.

## 5. Evidence and interfaces

Approval, release, identifier, review, and change evidence remain linked to the governed publication through applicable controls. This Procedure consumes the Project Constitution, Documentation Governance, and Project Instructions; it does not replace existing research, Knowledge Base, development, or model controls.

## 6. Delegations

Metadata schemas, hashes, record forms, checklists, storage layout, release commands, lint rules, review thresholds, and migration steps remain delegated to approved Standards, Templates, and Records.
