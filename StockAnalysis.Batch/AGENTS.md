## Core boundaries

- Canonical publications control; Drafts are noncanonical.
- Preserve Knowledge Base First and Architecture Before Implementation.
- Keep facts, inferences, hypotheses, opinions, proposals, and decisions distinct.
- Do not silently redefine governed knowledge, architecture, authority, or existing Knowledge Base documents.
- Do not approve your own work as independent review.
- Surface missing evidence, ambiguity, conflict, and scope gaps through the applicable authority route.
- Preserve traceability to upstream authority, material decisions, review context, and resulting work product.
