## Question

Can the FY2021–FY2025 Fuji Electric values currently identified as Semiconductor-segment sales by application: Automotive be treated as a definition-stable, issuer-internal annual observation chain?

## Primary-Source Facts

| Pair | Source-defined evidence | Fact-only interpretation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| FY2021 → FY2022 | FY2022 results, dated 2023-04-27, identify the Semiconductor application breakdown as Industrial, Magnetic Disk, and Automotive. Automotive is JPY73.1bn (FY2021) and JPY100.2bn (FY2022). The page footnote states: `Figures for FY2021 reflect the organizational restructuring conducted in FY2022.` | The issuer presented the FY2021 comparative on a basis reflecting the FY2022 reorganization. |
| FY2022 → FY2023 | FY2023 results, dated 2024-04-25, show Automotive JPY100.2bn (FY2022) and JPY125.6bn (FY2023) on presentation p. 9 / PDF p. 8. The document states, `Figures for FY2022 have been restated to reflect the reorganization of the former Power Generation segment,` on presentation p. 7 / PDF p. 6 and repeats that note in the FY2023 major-components order-trend context on presentation p. 11 / PDF p. 10. | The same FY2022 Automotive value appears in the consecutive application-sales comparison. The restatement wording names former Power Generation; no source statement located in this audit says that the Semiconductor Automotive row was restated. |
| FY2023 → FY2024 | FY2024 results, dated 2025-04-25, show `Sales` for Automotive of JPY125.6bn (FY2023) and JPY132.6bn (FY2024) within `Semiconductors`. | The FY2023 value is reproduced in a consecutive Semiconductor application-sales comparison. |
| FY2024 → FY2025 | FY2025 results, dated 2026-04-28, show `Sales` for Automotive of JPY132.6bn (FY2024) and JPY117.7bn (FY2025) within `Semiconductors` on presentation p. 10 / PDF p. 9. The document also states, `Figures for FY2024 performance reflect the business reorganization undertaken in the FY2025,` in its annual business-results-by-segment context on presentation pp. 7–9 / PDF pp. 6–8 and in quarterly-orders contexts on presentation pp. 13–14 / PDF pp. 12–13. The Semiconductor application-sales table itself does not repeat that footnote. | The FY2024 value is reproduced in a consecutive annual Semiconductor application-sales comparison. The audit has not located disclosure that assigns the FY2025 reorganization’s effect to the Semiconductor Automotive row; absence of a located statement is not proof of no effect. |

## Controlled Inference

**Inference (pending independent confirmation):** Each adjacent annual pair is displayed by Fuji Electric as `Semiconductors (Automotive)` / `Automotive` sales in a consecutive comparison. The original documents therefore support a *candidate* issuer-internal annual application-sales chain:

```text
FY2021  73.1
FY2022 100.2
FY2023 125.6
FY2024 132.6
FY2025 117.7
JPY billion
```

This does **not** establish cross-company comparability, volume demand, a causal relationship to vehicle production, an investment signal, or permission for point-in-time use.
