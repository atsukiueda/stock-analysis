## EVR-035 Review Correction Overlay

> **EVR-036 / EVR-037 table boundary:** Both records present FY2024 `Sanken Core` / `Automotive` JPY31,668m, but `EVR-036` is from `Sales by Market` and `EVR-037` is from `Net Sales by Market`. The source does not state that the tables are identical, mapped, continuous, or comparable. The equal value and shared label do not establish any such relationship.

> **Fact and arithmetic boundary:** `EVR-035` records JPY31,668m because that is the FY2026 source-presented FY2024 Automotive comparative value. The same source displays JPY55,562m, JPY20,821m, and JPY3,072m in the reclassification note; their subtraction yields JPY31,669m, not JPY31,668m. No rounding explanation, residual allocation, or reconciliation is inferred. The source-presented Automotive value controls this Draft record.
