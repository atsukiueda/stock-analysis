## Facts

- ROHM identifies SiC power devices, power diodes, power MOSFETs, and IGBTs as discrete-semiconductor products.
- Its Integrated Report states that the relevant products serve consumer, industrial-equipment, and automotive applications.
- ROHM describes recent EV-market growth as slow while expressing a medium- to long-term view of continuing automotive electrification; it also identifies PHEV and HEV applications in its SiC discussion.
- The official IR site provides financial reports, fact books, integrated reports, and financial-results briefing materials.
- **Draft source observations:** ROHM's FY2022–FY2025 results presentations report Automotive market sales of JPY172.5bn (FY2021), JPY213.0bn (FY2022), JPY229.4bn (FY2023), JPY223.8bn (FY2024), and JPY236.8bn (FY2025) (`EVR-022`, `EVR-021`, `EVR-023`, and `EVR-019`; raw record `6963_ROHMAutomotiveMarketSalesRawEvidence.v0.1-draft.md`). These are market classifications, not automotive-only semiconductor product revenue or SiC revenue. No comparability class is assigned; review status is Pending classification.

## Inference

ROHM is a high-priority source for automotive power and SiC context. Its FY2021–FY2025 Automotive market-sales source observations are useful research evidence, but must remain separated from automotive product revenue and from industry-demand realization until definition continuity is independently disposed.
