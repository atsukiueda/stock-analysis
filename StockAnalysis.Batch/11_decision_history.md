# Decision History

> **APPROVED AND RELEASED.** Navigation index for decision records; not a source of new decisions.

## Purpose

This index supports traceability to material project decisions, their authority, rationale, consequences, and current status. It does not restate, amend, or supersede a canonical decision record.

## Canonical decision sources

| Subject | Canonical location | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Existing Knowledge Base decisions | `KnowledgeBase/00_Project/DecisionLog.md` | Existing authority within stated scope |
| Architecture decision records | `KnowledgeBase/00_Project/ArchitectureDecisionRecords/` | Reserved decision-record location |
| Project-wide governing decisions | Applicable Project Constitution, approval record, or decision record | Follow linked authority |

## Decision index fields

Each future index entry should link to—not duplicate—the canonical record and identify: decision identifier, subject, authority, date, status, predecessor or successor relationship, and affected governing scope.

## Boundaries

Facts, rationale, consequences, and unresolved issues remain with the canonical decision record. A decision becomes effective only through its applicable authority and lifecycle controls. This index creates no decision, approval, or migration authority.
