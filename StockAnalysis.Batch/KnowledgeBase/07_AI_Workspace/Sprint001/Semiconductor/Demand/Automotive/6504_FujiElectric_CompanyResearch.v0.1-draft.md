## Facts

- Fuji Electric’s 2025 report describes automotive opportunities for power semiconductors and an expected increase in hybrid vehicles, while identifying EV growth as slower. **Evidence: primary-source item 1; issuer strategy context, not an industry forecast.**
- The report describes development of compact, lower-loss, high-reliability power-semiconductor modules for automotive use. **Evidence: primary-source item 1; product context, not application-sales allocation.**
- In its FY2025 results summary, Fuji Electric states that automotive-semiconductor sales declined because of reduced demand for power semiconductors for electrified vehicles and prior-year pricing revisions. **Evidence: EVR-007 / EVR-016, FY2025 results printed p. 10 / PDF p. 9.**
- The official financial-results archive provides FY2021 through FY2025 materials. **Evidence: primary-source item 4; archive presence only, not series comparability.**
- **Fact:** Fuji's FY2022–FY2025 results disclose a source-defined `Automotive` application-sales row within the Semiconductors segment: FY2021 JPY73.1bn, FY2022 JPY100.2bn, FY2023 JPY125.6bn, FY2024 JPY132.6bn, and FY2025 JPY117.7bn. See EVR-015–EVR-018. The FY2022 source states that FY2021 figures reflect organizational restructuring conducted in FY2022.

## Inference

**Inference:** Fuji Electric is a candidate for issuer-specific automotive-semiconductor demand context. The disclosed explanation shows why application sales must be interpreted with product-demand and pricing context rather than EV or vehicle volume alone. The current evidence does not establish inventory or capacity observations.

- **Fact:** Fuji Electric reports a Semiconductors segment; its FY2025 results table reports segment net sales of JPY237.4bn (`EVR-009` / `EVR-016`). This is an issuer segment, not automotive revenue.
- **Direct source observation, definition-controlled:** `EVR-015`–`EVR-018` record Semiconductor-segment sales by application: Automotive for FY2021–FY2025. This is not group-wide automotive revenue, a volume metric, or an approved comparable time series.
- **Issuer-specific commentary:** `EVR-007` records the FY2025 automotive-semiconductor sales commentary and its paired demand/price causes.
- **Comparability:** `Non-comparable` with automotive revenue of other issuers unless each source has the same scope and period.
