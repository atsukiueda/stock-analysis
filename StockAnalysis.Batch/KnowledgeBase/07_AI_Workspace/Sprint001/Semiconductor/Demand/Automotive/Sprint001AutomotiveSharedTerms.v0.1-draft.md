# Sprint001 — Automotive Semiconductor Shared Terms

> Draft terminology aid only. It creates no canonical glossary or taxonomy authority.

| Term | Working meaning | Excludes | Reference |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| End demand | Market/OEM-side vehicle production, sales, registrations, or published guidance, preserving source definition. | Semiconductor order, supplier revenue, or inventory by implication. | Value Chain §6 |
| Automotive semiconductor demand | Source-defined semiconductor supplier demand/revenue/order/guidance attributed to automotive scope. | General semiconductor revenue, unless the source defines automotive scope. | Evidence Register EVR-003–005, 007–008 |
| Tier 1 systems | Vehicle-system layer between OEM demand and semiconductor suppliers. | Direct supplier allocation without source evidence. | Value Chain §3 |
| Inventory adjustment | Difference between observed end-demand context and supplier operating state that may involve inventory. | A positive/negative demand conclusion from inventory alone. | Indicator Catalog §4; historical semantic-mapping assets |
| Candidate leading indicator | Observation public before a defined target and not yet validated for predictive contribution. | An approved model feature or causal signal. | Indicator Catalog §2 |
| Available-at | Earliest permitted use time derived from a publication event under a future approved operating convention. | Applicable period end date. | Evidence Matrix §7 |
| Comparability class | `Direct`, `Proxy`, or `Non-comparable`, based on documented definition/scope consistency. | A quality score or adoption decision. | Evidence Acquisition Matrix remediation |
| Pending classification | Draft lifecycle status: source observations are acquired but no independent comparability-class decision has been recorded. | A fourth comparability class, Catalog eligibility, or permission for downstream use. | Fuji raw evidence / review record |
