## 1. Purpose and scope

This Procedure defines the controlled method for observing governed model and decision-system health, handling concerns, preserving context, and closing incidents with traceable evidence. It does not define metrics, thresholds, alert configuration, remediation methods, deployment commands, investment risk limits, or investment decisions.

## 2. Principles

Monitoring and incident handling preserve continuous accountability: no silent incident, proportional response, evidence-preserving investigation, controlled communication, reversible containment, and traceable closure.

## 3. Procedure

1. Establish monitoring context: governed object, lifecycle state, validation disposition, explanation/traceability references, and applicable change context.
2. Receive a detected concern from automated, human, audit, or downstream signals.
3. Classify and triage proportionately, preserving evidence, metadata, references, and Records as distinct linked concepts.
4. Route escalation and communication through applicable constitutional authority and independent-review boundaries.
5. Route containment, suspension, withdrawal, rollback, or controlled change to the applicable Lifecycle and Model Change controls.
6. Preserve traceable investigation relationships for model drift, data-drift references, performance observations, decision consistency, explanation gaps, trace gaps, or lifecycle anomalies as applicable.
7. Close only when completion criteria, residual-risk consideration, closure evidence, and applicable decision record are present; route governed feedback to upstream knowledge or evidence improvement where applicable.

## 4. Interfaces and delegation

This Procedure consumes Lifecycle, Validation, Explainability, Change, Development, Documentation, and Investment references, and provides incident context to applicable controls. Metrics, thresholds, alerts, runbooks, forms, communications templates, and remediation methods remain delegated.
