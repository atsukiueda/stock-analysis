# Testing & Validation

> **Draft — not released.** Proposed Standard; not effective until independently reviewed, approved, and released.

## 1. Purpose and scope

This Standard defines project-level testing and validation direction for governed development, model, decision-system, and evidence-related work. It establishes responsibility boundaries and traceability expectations; it does not prescribe test cases, metrics, thresholds, tools, or execution commands.

## 2. Principles

Validation is evidence-based, proportionate to governed change and risk, independently reviewable where material, reproducible at the required level, and traceable to the work’s purpose and upstream authority. Passing one validation type does not imply fitness in another domain.

## 3. Validation boundaries

| Validation concern | Governing route |
|---|---|
| Software behavior and integration | Development Governance and Development Workflow |
| Model and decision-system fitness | Model & Decision-System Validation Standard |
| Investment or strategy fitness | Applicable future Investment Governance; distinct from model validation |
| Research evidence and claim fitness | Existing Research Policy and Research Writing Guide |
| Documentation conformance | Documentation Governance and Documentation Workflow |

## 4. Evidence and lifecycle

Testing and validation must preserve the applicable relationship among scope, upstream authority, method reference, observed result, disposition, reviewer context, and lifecycle or release routing. Evidence, metadata, references, and Records remain distinct; schemas are delegated.

## 5. Delegations

Test plans, cases, fixtures, datasets, metrics, thresholds, coverage, Backtest, Walk Forward, performance targets, test tools, CI configuration, and commands remain delegated to applicable lower-level Standards, Procedures, Templates, and Records.
