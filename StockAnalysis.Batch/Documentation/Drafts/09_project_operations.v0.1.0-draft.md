# Project Operations

> **Draft — not released.** Proposed Guide for long-term project continuity.

## Purpose

This guide supports long-term operation through clear ownership, governed handoff, current canonical documentation, and visible unresolved work. It does not define deployment, incident, security, personnel, or infrastructure procedures.

## Operating principles

Use canonical publications as the source of current authority. Preserve decisions, evidence, and handoff context. Escalate unresolved authority conflicts. Do not silently replace existing Knowledge Base authority.

## Operational guidance

- Start work from README, Project Constitution, Project Instructions, and the applicable domain control.
- Keep material work, decisions, reviews, releases, and changes traceable through applicable records.
- Use role and decision routing in the Constitution; an appointment does not grant unassigned authority.
- Maintain visible backlog, decision history, and migration status through their applicable canonical artifacts.
- Treat handoff as preservation of scope, authority, evidence, current state, unresolved issues, and next decision route.

## Boundaries

Detailed documentation, development, model, research, validation, incident, and release workflows remain governed by their applicable policies, standards, procedures, and records.
