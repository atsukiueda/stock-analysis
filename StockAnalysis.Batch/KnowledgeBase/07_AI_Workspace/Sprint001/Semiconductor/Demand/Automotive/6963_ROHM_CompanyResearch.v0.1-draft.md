## Facts

- ROHM identifies SiC power devices, power diodes, power MOSFETs, and IGBTs as discrete-semiconductor products.
- Its Integrated Report states that the relevant products serve consumer, industrial-equipment, and automotive applications.
- ROHM describes recent EV-market growth as slow while expressing a medium- to long-term view of continuing automotive electrification; it also identifies PHEV and HEV applications in its SiC discussion.
- The official IR site provides financial reports, fact books, integrated reports, and financial-results briefing materials.

## Inference

ROHM is a high-priority source for automotive power and SiC context. Its own automotive statements must be separated from quantitative automotive-demand realization because the exact revenue scope and time series require disclosure-by-disclosure verification.
