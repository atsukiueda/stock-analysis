# Review Checklists

> **Draft — not released.** Guide and reusable review prompts; not an approval standard.

## Purpose

These checklists help independent reviewers find defects consistently. They do not replace reviewer judgment, create approval authority, define thresholds, or allow an author to self-approve.

## Use

Select the applicable checklist, inspect evidence and governing sources, record findings with scope and recommendation, and preserve the disposition. A checklist is a prompt set, not proof that an artifact is defect-free.

## Universal checklist

- Is the document class, status, owner, scope, and governing authority explicit?
- Does it conflict with a higher authority or silently expand its scope?
- Are facts, inferences, hypotheses, proposals, and decisions distinguishable?
- Are material claims, decisions, and dependencies traceable?
- Are exceptions, changes, and lifecycle references routed to the applicable control?
- Is the artifact maintainable, readable, and free of competing authority?

## Documentation and governance checklist

- Is canonical publication distinct from Draft material?
- Are Policy, Standard, Procedure, Guide, Template, and Record responsibilities separated?
- Does it preserve existing Knowledge Base authority absent approved migration?
- Are roles and decision authority consistent with the Project Constitution?

## Workflow and procedure checklist

- Are handoffs, independent review, approval, release, exceptions, and closure routed clearly?
- Does the Procedure avoid hidden commands, schemas, thresholds, or implementation methods?
- Are evidence and record relationships stated without inventing record formats?

## Model and development checklist

- Does work preserve Knowledge Base First and Architecture Before Implementation where applicable?
- Are model validation and investment validation distinct?
- Are explainability, traceability, lifecycle, change, and monitoring interfaces consistent?
- Does development work avoid taking authority over research, data, model, or investment domains?

## Finding format

Record severity, observed evidence, affected scope, governing reference, recommendation, and disposition link. Use the applicable workflow for review records and remediation.
