# Personas

> **Draft — not released.** Guide to project roles and participation; it creates no authority.

## Purpose

This guide helps participants understand how to enter the project, what information they need, and where authority resides. A persona describes a participation perspective; the Project Constitution remains authoritative for roles, appointment, and decision authority.

## Project Director

Sets project direction, priorities, architecture and Knowledge Base direction, and final substantive decisions. Uses the Constitution and Decision Authority Matrix to appoint, decide, approve, or reject within assigned authority.

## Documentation participant

Includes authors, Documentation Team roles, editors, and reviewers. Uses Documentation Governance, Documentation Workflow, and Review Checklists; preserves canonicality and does not silently alter existing Knowledge Base authority.

## Research and Knowledge Base participant

Uses Knowledge Base Rules, Research Policy, and Research Writing Guide. Preserves evidence, claim boundaries, provenance, and upstream knowledge authority.

## Development participant

Includes human developers, Codex, other AI agents, and external contractors acting under assigned roles. Uses Development Governance and Development Workflow; implements governed knowledge and architecture rather than redefining them.

## Model and decision-system participant

Uses Model Governance and the applicable Control Family artifacts. Preserves lifecycle, validation, explainability, traceability, change, and monitoring boundaries; distinguishes model validation from investment validation.

## Independent reviewer

Finds defects independently of authorship. Uses the applicable domain control and Review Checklists, records evidence-based findings, and does not treat review as automatic endorsement.

## External participant

Includes external reviewers or contractors. Must have an assigned scope, declared independence or engagement constraints where applicable, and no authority beyond their appointment.

## Boundary

This guide does not assign roles, approve work, define procedures, or replace the Role Organization and Decision Authority Matrix in the Project Constitution.
